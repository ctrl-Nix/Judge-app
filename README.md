# Judge — The Digital Guillotine

**Judge** is not a chatbot. It is a high-engagement, psychologically-driven roast engine designed to dismantle egos and deliver cold, digital truth. Built with a "Cyber-Glass Noir" aesthetic, it utilizes advanced AI to analyze your files, your images, and your life.

## 💀 Features

- **Contextual Omniscience**: The Judge knows your local time, your battery percentage, and your boredom levels.
- **Multimodal Vision**: Upload images to have your physical reality roasted by Gemini 2.0 Flash.
- **Sin Registry (Archive)**: A local database that records every insult thrown at you. Your failures are now permanent.
- **Hacker Slash Commands**: 
    - `/aukaat` — Immediate final verdict.
    - `/mercy` — Mockery for the weak.
    - `/clear` — Registry wipe (with commentary).
    - `/identity` — Hierarchy clarification.
- **The Silent Treatment**: Low-effort users are ignored and locked out of the interface.
- **Global Heartbeat**: A live (simulated) tracker of egos being dismantled in real-time.
- **Aukaat Card**: A high-contrast, shareable verdict card for viral shame.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Core**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS (Cyber-Glass UI), ES6+ JavaScript.
- **AI**: Gemini 2.0 Flash (Multimodal Roast Engine).
- **Libraries**: PDF.js (PDF parsing), Mammoth.js (Docx parsing).
- **Deployment**: Optimized for Vercel Serverless Functions.

## 🚀 Deployment

1. Clone the repository.
2. Set your `GEMINI_API_KEY` in environment variables.
3. Run `npm install` and `npm run dev`.
4. Enter at your own risk.
